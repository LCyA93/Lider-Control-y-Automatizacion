What is a Power BI Odoo Connector?

The Power BI Odoo Connector bridges Microsoft Power BI and Odoo, allowing you to transform live ERP data into interactive dashboards, business reports, KPIs, and executive analytics without complex integrations.

What Power BI Means

Power BI is Microsoft's business intelligence platform that turns raw business data into meaningful dashboards, reports, and visual analytics.

Instead of exporting spreadsheets every day, Power BI connects directly with Odoo and automatically refreshes your reports using scheduled or on-demand synchronisation.

What the Connector Does

Without It

  • Export Excel files manually
  • Build reports repeatedly
  • Copy data between systems
  • Outdated dashboards
  • No automation

With It

  • Automatic data synchronisation
  • Real-time dashboards
  • Scheduled refresh
  • Executive reporting
  • Centralized analytics

Three Things to Understand

01
Your Odoo data becomes business intelligence. Transform Sales, Purchase, Inventory, Accounting, CRM, Manufacturing and HR data into interactive reports.
02
You control what gets synchronised. Choose models, fields, companies, records, refresh schedules and datasets.
03
Works with the complete Microsoft Power BI ecosystem. Built to work across the full Microsoft Power BI ecosystem, including Microsoft Fabric.
